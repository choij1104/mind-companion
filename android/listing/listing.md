## Full description (EN)
Mind Companion is a simple, private memory aid for older adults and the people who care for them.

Press the big microphone and ask, in your own words:
• "What day is it today?"
• "Did I take my medicine?"
• "Who is my doctor?"
• "Where did I put my passport?"
The app answers out loud from what you have saved — nothing is looked up on the internet.

WHAT IT DOES
• Medicine — a daily checklist with big squares to tap when you have taken each one, and optional reminders while the app is open
• Contacts — family, doctor and pharmacy, each one tap from a phone call
• Memory notes — write or say where things are, then ask for them by voice later
• My mind — a one-tap daily mood, a two-question check-in, and a summary page you can show at counseling
• Help — a big button to call 911, call family, share where you are by text message, and show your conditions, allergies and blood type to paramedics
• Daily routine, larger text, Simple Mode, and caregiver tools (4-week report, CSV, backup and restore)

FOUR LANGUAGES
English, 한국어, Español, 日本語 — the screen and the spoken answers both switch.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone. The Google Play version works without an internet connection. Voice input uses your phone's own speech-recognition service; you can always type instead.

IMPORTANT
Mind Companion is a memory aid, not a medical device, and it does not give medical advice. Medicine reminders sound only while the app is open and must not be the only way you remember to take medicine. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. In an emergency call 911. Mental health crisis: call or text 988.

Made by HAKOYA LLC, Texas.
